# The Loop — Tessera's Operating Doctrine

*Named by Casey, 2026-09-27: "a complicated GAN-like loop — we shape the solution to the ideal through shaping the ideal through what we believe is really possible."*

## The cast

| GAN role | Tessera organ | Honesty contract |
|---|---|---|
| Generator | Ideation crew: council voices, foresight lanes, stories, thought experiments | Must project from INSIDE the future; must not restate the present |
| Discriminator | The Skeptic voice, kill-criteria, the no-go ledger | Must propose falsification experiments, not just objections |
| Oracle | The hardware. The 4050, the lab probes, the paste test | Cannot be flattered. Its "no" outranks every document |
| Data refresh | Scouts: repos, papers, culture, our own org's organs | Contradicting evidence is the highest-value input |

## The cycle

1. **Dream** — stories, ideation, foresight (far/mid/near). Prose is a first-class artifact.
2. **Shape** — the ideal gets constrained by what we believe is possible (council, skeptic, architecture).
3. **Diffuse** — thought experiments decompose into the smallest hardware-runnable probe (the "diffusion" from narrative to experiment: one script, <10 min, honest logging).
4. **Run** — experiments on our silicon. Most are no-gos. **The ledger keeps them**: a no-go with its error and what it rules out is worth more than an unexamined maybe (tapestry doctrine — negative results map the edges).
5. **Some breakthrough goes** — rare, load-bearing, and they reshape the ideal itself.
6. **Re-scout** — every go/no-go changes what's worth scouting next. Back to 1.

## Standing rules

- **No ideal survives untested.** Any foresight claim older than one cycle without a probe attached gets flagged, not believed.
- **No experiment without an ideal.** A probe must name which future claim it serves — else it's metric-chasing.
- **Mode-collapse watch:** if ideation keeps producing the same shapes, force a contradiction pass (scout evidence that argues *against* the current ideal is mandatory reading).
- **The ledger is the dataset.** Every cycle, `lab/` notes and kill-criteria outcomes get folded back into IDEATION/FORESIGHT — the loop's weights.
