# Scout — The Culture Moment (2024-2026 text-mode landscape, 2026-09-27)

*Live web-verified scan. Where a claim is pattern-level rather than one viral moment, it says so.*

## The wave is named and buyable
- "Technical Mono / Code Brutalism" + "Lo-fi Pixel & Dither" named in 2025-26 design trend forecasts (Studio2AM, Imajery) — brands crave technical authenticity post-AI. Shutterstock sells an ASCII stock-footage category ("club visuals"); Stellantis publishes ASCII craft deep-dives. **Mainstream brands are circling the category — own it before they do.**
- ASCIICraft: 150+ ASCII movie/music-video conversions + an active ASCII lyric-video genre on YouTube. Appetite proven; supply of *good* text-mode video is not. **Honest gap: no blockbuster ASCII music video yet — the lane is open.**

## Terminal-core is identity, not nostalgia
- Severance S2: CRT-green Lumon terminals = prestige-TV iconography. Ghostty (Dec 2024): a *terminal emulator* went genuinely viral. Berkeley Mono: waitlisted luxury monospace. neofetch's funeral → fastfetch; r/unixporn ricing as self-expression.
- Teletext/demoscene institutionalized as *heritage*: edit.tf browser editor, Dan Farrimond's teletext art practice, demoscene on UNESCO national heritage lists (Finland 2012, Germany 2022), Revision still packing halls. Heritage status is what makes text-mode usable as high culture.

## Creator culture
- Terminal Shop (`ssh terminal.shop`) — funded startup whose brand IS the terminal: constraint-as-delight works as commerce.
- Caves of Qud 1.0 (Dec 2024): 17-year text-mode roguelike hits 1.0 to major coverage — "ugly" deep games over-perform. Devlog audiences love visible constraint-solving → **Tessera build-in-public devlogs have a ready-made audience.**
- Faceless-channel economy boomed; text-mode is the ultimate privacy-preserving visual. Pitch it as the *ethical* faceless format: privacy by aesthetic, not evasion.

## The AI angle — slop fatigue is the tailwind
- "Slop" = word of the year (Merriam-Webster/ADS 2025), mentions ~9× since 2024. Preference for gen-AI creator content collapsed 60% → 26% (2023→2025, Billion Dollar Boy via Digiday); brands explicitly requesting "messiness." Coca-Cola AI ads and the Ghibli-filter flood drew backlash.
- Text-mode is the visual opposite of AI video's uncanny gloss — and LLMs are notoriously *bad* at ASCII, so coherent glyphs function as **proof-of-human**. Good Internet: "ASCII art is an art born from limitations."

## Positioning
**Authored, not generated; readable, not rendered.** Every frame checkable character-by-character, constraint worn as craft, in the demoscene/teletext lineage culture just enshrined as heritage. Sell the hand, not the nostalgia: text-mode isn't retro, it's *legible* — the one video format where you can see every pixel is deliberate. That legibility is also the moat: it's the medium AI is worst at faking.

**The hook: "Video you can read — proof a human made every frame."**
