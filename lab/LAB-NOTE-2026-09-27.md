## 3. Experiment: ELEPHANT-ON-GLYPHS — **works**

Fits (non-overlapping windows; A also from overlapping where noted):

| room | κ̂ | ρ̄ | warmth_vmf | κ 95% CI | μ_se |
|------|-----|------|-----------|----------|------|
| A-calm-slow | **160.1** | 0.981 | **+0.688** | [60, 500] | 0.047 |
| B-busy-fast | 60.8 | 0.951 | −0.068 | [22, 500] | 0.065 |
| C-calm-fast | 55.1 | 0.947 | +0.041 | [20, 500] | 0.068 |
| D-busy-slow | 86.1 | 0.966 | +0.186 | [32, 500] | 0.059 |

- **Separation:** all 6 room-pairs pass the drift gate with `real: True`. d_μ: A→B **1.029**, A→C 0.964, A→D 0.784; within the busy family B→C 0.189, B→D 0.432, C→D 0.472. kl_sym: A-vs-others 75–115; within-family 2–16. The walk from the still room to the busy room is enormous on S⁶.
- **Sauna/plunge:** all gaps positive, scaling with true room distance: +0.528 (A/B), +0.468 (A/C), +0.302 (A/D), +0.093 (B/D), +0.106 (C/D), +0.014 (B/C — the two busiest rooms, nearly the same elephant). Same-room cosine 0.97–0.99.
- **κ tracks temperature coarsely:** κ̂ order A > D > B > C vs change order A < D < C < B — monotone except B/C swap, and that swap is inside overlapping CIs. The identifiable statement is d_log_kappa(A→B) = **−0.97**: walking into the busy room costs ~an e-fold of concentration. κ CIs are wide at N=16 windows in d=7 — honest, and a reason to pool more windows in production.
- **Booked finding — prior sign inversion:** in glyph space the *still* room is both warmest (mood=persistence mapping) AND tightest (κ 160). The v3 folk prior "cold = high κ" does not transfer as a law; the geometry transfers, the sign is a property of the room class.

**Verdict (3 sentences):** It worked — elephant's estimator, deadband gate, and contrast metrics read the four glyph rooms as four different rooms with the geometry they should have, on a modality they were never built for. This means Tessera gets a room-temperature sense organ almost for free: a `GlyphSpace` adapter (frames → 7 hand-crafted dial readings → existing vmf pipeline) makes every ASCII clip a room the fleet can feel, and quilt cells can log the edges via `record_with`. Next experiment: replace the hand-crafted dials with windows of the `gpu/` v0 room encoder's embeddings (via `~/venvs/elephant-gpu`) on real chiaroscuro webcam streams, with the speaker/cast-heldout control the spec makes mandatory.

## 4. Experiment: JEV-MACRO probe — **works, with a design confirmation**

`jev_macro_probe.py` — 512 frames, all 130,816 pairs; macro = 4×4 mean-pooled luminance (20-dim flattened 20×6 grid), repr = standardized 6-feature frame vector, glyph = raw 1920-dim grid.

| distance pair | Pearson (all) | Spearman (all) | within-room | cross-room |
|---------------|--------------|----------------|-------------|------------|
| d_repr vs d_macro | 0.354 | 0.336 | 0.385 | 0.254 |
| d_glyph vs d_macro | **0.824** | **0.891** | 0.773 | **0.961** |
| d_repr vs d_glyph | 0.362 | 0.412 | 0.269 | 0.341 |

And the decisive one, per-frame: **corr(repr-change, macro-delta) = 0.966** while corr(luminance, macro-delta) = 0.187. The pooled grid's *temporal* delta is nearly lossless for the temperature axis; its *static* luminance is useless for it.

**Verdict (3 sentences):** The probe ran (after one broadcasting bug, fixed) and gives the pyramid contract a cheap, measurable target: a dumb 4×4 mean-pool already preserves glyph distance structure at ρ≈0.82–0.96, so a hand-crafted E0 is a legitimate floor for testing d(E0(x), L0) ≤ ε before any training. More importantly it *confirms the charter's L0 design from below*: the beat/energy axis (macro temporal delta) carries the temperature structure (r=0.97) while static pooled luminance does not (r=0.19) — L0 must be flow+beat, not a picture summary, exactly as the scientist wrote. Next experiment: repeat this probe with real chiaroscuro frames and L0 = 40×20 flow+beat tokens from RAFT-lite, correlating contract violation ε against the elephant's per-edge d_μ — i.e., make "the room-sense survives the pyramid" the regression test for the render pipeline.
